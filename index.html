About Kivesi Logistics

East Africa's Trusted
Logistics Partner

Founded in 2024, Kivesi Logistics was built on a clear mission: to provide businesses across East and Central Africa with logistics solutions that are fast, reliable, and genuinely convenient. We understand the unique challenges of cross-border trade in this region.

From Dar es Salaam to Kinshasa, Lilongwe to Maputo — our network spans some of the most dynamic commercial corridors in Africa. Whether you're shipping construction materials, retail goods, or manufacturing supplies, Kivesi delivers with precision and professionalism.
