About Us

Diversified Excellence Across East Africa

Kivesi Investment (T) Limited is a diversified and dynamic company dedicated to providing high-quality services and products across East Africa and beyond. With a strong commitment to reliability, efficiency and customer satisfaction, we operate in multiple sectors that contribute to economic growth and development within the region.

From logistics and transportation to food supply, events, real estate, spare parts and procurement, we provide practical solutions tailored to each customer’s requirements.

Food Supply
