About Us

Built for dependable regional service

A diversified company designed to move business forward with dependable execution.

Kivesi Investment (T) Limited provides practical support across multiple sectors with a strong focus on reliability, speed, customer care and regional market understanding. The business supports clients with real-world solutions that match daily operational needs across East and Southern Africa.

Vision

To be a trusted regional partner known for dependable service, responsive operations and sustainable business growth across diverse sectors.

Mission

To deliver quality logistics and support services through practical coordination, customer-focused execution and strong regional reach.

Core Values

  • Reliability in day-to-day service delivery.
  • Operational efficiency backed by clear communication.
  • Customer-first support built around practical solutions.
  • Integrity, accountability and long-term relationships.
