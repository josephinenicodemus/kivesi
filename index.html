About Us

Built for Reliable Regional Service

Kivesi Investment (T) Limited is a diversified Tanzanian company delivering practical logistics, supply and business support solutions. Since 2018, our work has been guided by reliability, operational discipline and direct customer service.

Our Vision

To become a trusted regional partner recognised for dependable logistics, responsible growth and service excellence across East and Southern Africa.

Our Mission

To provide timely, safe and customer-focused transport, supply and investment services through disciplined operations and strong regional relationships.
